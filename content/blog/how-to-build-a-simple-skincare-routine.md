Many people think they need 10 products for healthy skin, but a simple routine starts with a cleanser to remove dirt and oil, a moisturizer to keep skin hydrated, and sunscreen as the final daytime step to protect against sun damage. Consistency matters more than product count — start simple and give skin time to adjust.

A good approach is to keep your routine focused and repeatable. In the morning, cleanse gently, apply moisturizer, and finish with sunscreen. At night, cleanse again and follow with moisturizer so your skin can recover while you sleep.

The most important habit is showing up every day. When you keep your skincare routine simple, it becomes easier to maintain and easier to stick with over time.
